Network

Off by default. QRC needs no DNS — the QR carries a raw address and pins a self-signed certificate, which our own clients verify. Turn this on only to support third-party IRC clients that refuse self-signed certificates: your address is published to Duck DNS and the certificate you paste below is served instead.
